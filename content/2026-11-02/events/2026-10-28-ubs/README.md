# Inside Equity Research: UBS, fintech and careers in equity markets

- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Place:** Room 0A04, ESADE Sant Cugat
- **Type:** Speaker
- **Departments:** Private Markets (Fintech), Corporate Relations
- **Speaker:** Jonathan Glele Ahanhanzo, former Equity Research Analyst at UBS (FinTech & Payments, EMEA TMT team), now MSc Development Economics at the University of Oxford
- **Key points:** Inside UBS: how equity research is structured and what day-to-day work in TMT looks like; Breaking into markets: advice on applications, interviews and pursuing equity research roles; Fintech & TMT: current market trends and the forces shaping the sector
- **Format:** Small group, speaker on video, members together in Room 0A04
- **Time not shown:** 17:00 (Wednesday 28 October)

## Text for the site

A small-group session with Jonathan Glele Ahanhanzo, former Equity Research Analyst at UBS covering FinTech & Payments in the EMEA TMT team, now pursuing an MSc in Development Economics at the University of Oxford. How equity research is structured and what day-to-day work in TMT looks like, advice on applications, interviews and pursuing equity research roles, and current market trends in fintech and TMT. An opportunity for EMFC members to ask questions and hear first-hand perspectives on equity research and fintech.
