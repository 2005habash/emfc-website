# Exploring Careers in Finance: Alumni Roundtables (with ESADE Careers)

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Place:** Auditorium, ESADE
- **Type:** Networking
- **Departments:** Marketing, Corporate Relations, Private Markets (Private Equity table)
- **Time not shown:** 13:00 to 14:00
- **Occasion:** Career Consulting Day, run with ESADE Careers
- **Tables:** Investment Banking, Private Equity, Venture Capital, Fintech and Sustainable Investments, one alumnus each, each hosted by an EMFC member
- **Private equity table:** Moderated by Fabian Luis Seher, with Miquel Petit of Abac Capital
- **Guests:** Investment Banking: Luis Vega, Investment Banking Associate, XP Advisory; Private Equity: Miquel Petit, Private Equity Associate, Abac Capital; Venture Capital: Miguel Bowden, Portfolio Management, InnoEnergy; Fintech: Martin Nicholls, Team Lead Enterprise, Frekuent; Sustainable Finance: Cristina Costa, Sustainability Lead, Kara
- **Published as:** Finance Roundtable. Five sectors. Five Esade alumni. One room full of questions. (EMFC x ESADE Careers, LinkedIn recap)

## Text for the site

EMFC’s first open event, run with ESADE Careers. Five tables, one per area of finance, where students could talk directly with alumni about what working in each field is really like. Students rotated between sectors to ask alumni what the job is really like, and how they got there.
