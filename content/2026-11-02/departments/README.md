# Department content and feedback

What each department sent for the website. There is one folder per department, numbered in the same order as `people/`. Each folder has:

- `README.md`: everything the department sent, organised by page and topic, nothing shortened. Each change shows the current text next to the new one.
- `reports.json` and `files/`: their reports and documents (PDFs), with the page they belong on, when they sent any.
- `originals/`: the messages and files exactly as received, with a full-text copy of each Word file.

Events are not here: several departments sent the same events, so they are merged in `events/` at the top of the package, one folder per event with its photos, videos and Marketing's published recap. All reports are also listed in `reports.json` at the top. Private Markets has an extra file, `desks.json`, with the content of its four desk pages.

## What VPs and Heads were asked

Vasco sent this to every VP and Head on 28 October, with the deadline of Sunday 1 November:

> Here's the new website (still a preview) and the current one, so you can compare. You're responsible for your department's pages. Go through them and check that every text says what you want: the description, what your desk does, names, titles, events and reports. Anything you'd change or add, send it to me. Compare both versions too. If there's something on the current site you'd like to keep, or something you'd like to see done differently on the new one, tell me. Photos will come from the member form, so no need to comment on those. After this round we're not changing it again, so make sure it's exactly how you want it.
