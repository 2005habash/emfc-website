# Archive

Retired parts of the site, kept so they can come back. Nothing in this folder is built, served, linted
or kept up to date: the build only reads `src/` and `public/`, and ESLint and Prettier skip `archive/`.

**Leave this folder out of site-wide changes.** Renames, copy rules, design changes, dependency updates
and searches across the site do not need to touch it. Whoever restores something from here brings it
up to date at that point.

| Folder | What it was | Retired |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `apply/` | The application flow (department one-pagers and the application form) | September 2026, after applications closed |
| `extra-pages/` | The hidden fun pages: `/zaid`, `/kostya` and `/puzzle` | 10 November 2026, no longer needed |
